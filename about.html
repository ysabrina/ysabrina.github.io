About Me

I'm a graduate-standing student at the University of Michigan School of Information. I specialize in UX design and research, but I have a multidisciplinary background in digital art, graphic design, and music composition and performance. I'm interested in making designs both accesible and delightful and I prioritize digital ethics in my professional work.

Aside from art and design, my other hobbies include playing piano and guitar, DJing, building custom PCs and keyboards, and raising chickens as pets and for eggs. Seriously, ask me about that.
